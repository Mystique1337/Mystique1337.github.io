About Me

At the intersection of AI, Energy, and Transformation.

I am a Process/Chemical Engineer and an AI Engineer driven to solve complex problems. My academic journey is a unique blend of two worlds: a foundational degree in Chemical & Process Engineering from the University of Lagos, combined with a degree in Applied Artificial Intelligence from IU, Germany. This dual perspective allows me to understand both the physical processes that power our world and the intelligent systems that can optimize them. My education has been backed by four national scholarships, wins in competitions, and various awards.

My core focus is on building process-first AI automations. I use a suite of tools to turn inefficient manual workflows into scalable, intelligent systems. Whether it's for energy, manufacturing, or tech, I thrive on identifying bottlenecks and shipping reliable automations that deliver measurable results.

My experience across leading companies like BlackCod Group (Bluebulb), CatalyzU (TechStars '23), Curacel (YC '22), TotalEnergies, Transcorp Power Plc, Nestlé, Clean Tech Hub, and NitHub reveals a consistent pattern: I leverage AI, automation, and engineering to improve and transform operations and focus on delivering tangible, measurable results.

But technology is only a tool; my real passion lies in multiplying its impact through people. Leadership, for me, is a platform for service and building communities. This conviction has driven me to take on roles such as President of the UNILAG Energy Club, where I led a team to achieve over 600% growth; VP of the Nigerian Society of Chemical Engineers (UNILAG), where I supported over 300 students; and Sponsorship Lead for GDSC UNILAG, securing over ₦5M for student tech projects. Through Project KUJUA, an initiative I founded, we have provided career mentorship to over 10,000 students in rural communities. My commitment to leadership and innovation has been recognized through global programs like the Millennium Fellowship, Student Energy, and the Developer's Foundry Fellowship (AI/ML).

Looking ahead, my vision is to become a business and global leader dedicated to building AI-powered solutions that accelerate the energy transition and support the growth of climate-tech. I want to be at the forefront of creating a more sustainable and equitable future.

I am always eager to connect with fellow innovators, founders, and leaders in the AI, energy, and climate-tech spaces. If you are passionate about building solutions that matter and are looking to collaborate on transformative projects, please feel free to reach out.

"Remember, one can chase a thousand, but with AI, one can chase a million."
