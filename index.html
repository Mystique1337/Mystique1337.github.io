About

Engineering first, AI where it earns its place

I am an AI/ML & Automation Engineer with a Chemical/Process Engineering background. I have built systems across fintech, insurtech, energy, FMCG, power, oil & gas and multi-subsidiary corporate operations.

That background changes the questions I ask before introducing AI: Is it more reliable? Is the output measurable? Can the workflow be audited? What fails safely? What should remain deterministic?

I am most interested in reliable LLM systems, agentic automation, multimodal AI and applied machine learning for complex real-world operations.
